# Stellar Cartography 0.3.0

Plot routes, see the galaxy's regions the way the in-game galaxy map names
them, and keep your friends and your fleet carrier on the map. The galaxy
now sits in intergalactic space, and its glow no longer follows where
players have been.

## New

- **Route plotting.** A Route panel under the search box (O) plots routes on
  Spansh: neutron routes (jump range and efficiency), fuel-exact galaxy
  routes using the ship from your journals, engineering included, and
  fleet carrier routes with tritium stops, several destinations and squadron
  carriers. The route draws on the map as its own layer, the waypoint list
  follows your jumps, and the next system can be copied for the in-game
  galaxy map after each jump. The route survives a restart.
- **Galactic regions.** Layers → Galactic regions (Shift+7) draws the 42
  codex regions as an atlas: their edges as hairlines on the galactic plane
  and each region's name inside it. The bottom-left readout names the region
  the view is centred on, and a system's details list its region. Under it,
  Mapping Project names adds the Galactic Mapping Project's 64 names for
  parts of the galaxy, such as the Abyssal Plain, Mare Desperationis and the
  Local Bubble. Both maps ship with the app. The layer starts off.
- **Colour systems by star class.** Layers → Colour systems by → Star class
  colours each populated system by its main star, hottest to coolest: O, B,
  A and F in blue, then G, K, M and brown dwarfs in orange. Neutron stars,
  white dwarfs, black holes and other types off the main sequence are grey
  and drawn bigger. Click a class in the legend to hide or show it.
- **Friends on the map.** Settings → Friends takes commander names and pins
  each one's last known position from EDSM, on its own layer. A friend shows
  up only if they upload their journals to EDSM with a public flight log,
  and only as fresh as their last upload. Names from your in-game friends
  list and wing are suggested as you type.
- **Your fleet carrier on the map.** Your own carrier is pinned with its
  name, read from your journals, and moves when a booked jump departs;
  hovering it says where it's jumping next. Docked aboard it, you go where
  it goes, even when it jumps while you're logged off.
- **Intergalactic backdrop.** Thousands of distant galaxies in clusters and
  filaments, with no foreground stars. Andromeda and Triangulum are in their
  true directions, and the Magellanic Clouds sit at their real distances and
  tilts, so they shift and turn as you fly. Layers → Distant galaxies
  (Shift+6) turns it off.

## Changed

- **Map markers.** You, your friends, your home system, your squadron
  capital and your carrier are LCARS block pins standing beside their stars.
  Pins that would overlap stack up instead of hiding each other. Your pin and
  your carrier's stay on the map whatever the layers. Megaships, the
  expedition carriers and carrier hubs are drawn as ships seen from above.
- **Home and Squadron go where you choose.** Settings → Jump buttons sets and
  clears them, and each button stays hidden until it has a system.
- **A truer galaxy glow.** The glow no longer doubles up where the catalogue
  already has stars, so the bubble and the busy routes don't stand out with a
  hard orange edge. Its density map no longer follows exploration either:
  the arms carry on through unexplored space and reach the galaxy's edge.
  Seen side on, the bulge fades out instead of ending in a flat top. The map
  is a little darker overall; Settings → Display → Brightness brings it back
  up.

## Upgrading from 0.2.0

Install over 0.2.0. Your settings and the data folder live outside the
install folder, so they carry over.

- **Star class colours** need one update of the populated systems (about 5
  minutes, mostly download). The layer offers it, or use Settings → Update
  from Spansh….
- **The new galaxy glow** comes with the next catalogue rebuild (Settings →
  Rebuild from EDSM…). From a source checkout, `cargo run --release -p ingest
  -- --maps --out <data folder>` redraws it from the existing build in about
  a minute, with no download.
- **Home and Squadron** no longer point at fixed systems. Choose them in
  Settings → Jump buttons.

## Install

Download `StellarCartography_0.3.0_x64-setup.exe` and run it. It installs for
your user only, on Windows 10 or 11 with WebView2. The installer isn't
signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn about it: choose More info → Run
anyway.

On first run the app offers to download and build the catalogue (about
3.4 GB streamed, about 10 GB kept, 10–15 minutes), or to use a build you
already have.
