    Stand by
    Drag to rotate · right-drag to pan · scroll to zoom · click a star or marker for details

    Set up the galaxy catalogue

    StellarCartography draws every system in EDSM's catalogue, so it keeps its own copy. It's built once from EDSM's nightly dump; rebuild it later from Settings to pick up new systems.

    Download and build

    Streams the latest dump (about 3.4 GB, not kept) and builds about 10 GB of data. Takes 10–15 minutes.

    Already built?

    Use the folder from an earlier build (it holds tiles and galaxy.sqlite).

    Settings

    Galaxy catalogue

    Downloads the latest dump (about 3.4 GB) and rebuilds the catalogue, which takes 10–15 minutes. The map stays usable meanwhile. The new copy needs about 10 GB of free space until it replaces the old one.

    Journal folder

    Jump buttons

    The Home and Squadron buttons under the search box fly to these systems. Type a name and pick it from the list. Each button shows once it has a system.

    System details

    Clicking a star shows its factions, Powerplay and stations. Systems you've visited use your journal; others are looked up online, falling back to the other site if the first is down. Spansh has Powerplay and landing pads, EDSM doesn't. Answers are cached for an hour.

    Friends

      Marks each commander's last known position on the map (Layers → Friends), from EDSM. Only commanders who upload their journals to EDSM (with EDMC or similar) and leave their flight log public can be found, and a position is only as fresh as their last upload. Names seen on your in-game friends list are suggested as you type.

      Populated systems

      Downloads Spansh's populated-systems dump (, not kept) and keeps about 17 MB of it for the map layers. Takes about 5 minutes; the map stays usable meanwhile.

      Colours the map by population, allegiance, government, economy, security, Powerplay or a minor faction (Layers → Colour systems by). Powerplay moves weekly; update whenever you like.

      Points of interest

      Downloads EDAstro's catalogues (, not kept, almost all of it codex reports) and keeps about 2 MB of places. Takes a few minutes; the map stays usable meanwhile.

      Guardian and Thargoid sites from players' codex reports, carrier hubs from the fleet carrier list, and megaships, nebulae and sights from the Galactic Exploration Catalog and EDSM's Galactic Mapping Project. Find them under Places.

      Updates

      Nothing downloads until you click Rebuild or Update in the reminder.

      Display

      A bigger point budget shows more stars at once but costs more GPU time; 2M suits integrated graphics.

      Keyboard

      Fly the map, jump to places, switch layers and open panels from the keyboard. Every key can be changed.

      Keyboard

      Click a key, then press the new one. Esc cancels; Backspace clears it.
